Who We Are

Engineering discipline meets flight passion

ABD RC is a technical services provider specializing in RC aircraft, UAV, FPV and RC vehicle engineering — from first concept through flight-tested delivery.

Our Mission

Precision engineering, made accessible

To deliver professional-grade RC and UAV engineering — design, fabrication, electronics and flight testing — with the rigor of aerospace practice and the accessibility of a dedicated technical workshop.

Our Vision

A trusted technical partner for flight

To be a dependable engineering partner for hobbyists, professional pilots, training organizations and commercial operators who need platforms that perform exactly as specified.

Engineering Philosophy

Every build starts with the physics, not the parts bin

We treat power system, airframe, electronics and control setup as one integrated system — not a collection of separately-sourced components.

Design First

Requirements and mission profile define the platform before a single part is sourced.

Verify, Don't Assume

CG, control throws and power systems are measured and tested, never eyeballed.

Built to Maintain

Platforms are built for serviceability, not just first flight.

Safety by Default

Structured protocols govern every stage, from bench testing to maiden flight.

Our Workshop

A dedicated engineering & fabrication space

Our workshop combines a design bench, an electronics bench, and in-house 3D printing, CNC and composite fabrication — so builds move from CAD file to flight-ready hardware without third-party delays.

  • CAD & simulation workstation
  • Electronics bench with diagnostic equipment
  • 3D printing, CNC and composite fabrication
  • Dedicated flight-test field access
