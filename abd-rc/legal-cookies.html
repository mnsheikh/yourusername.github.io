1. What This Covers

This site uses browser local storage (not traditional tracking cookies) to remember your theme preference (dark/light mode) on this device. No personally identifiable information is stored this way.

2. Essential Storage

Theme preference is stored using localStorage and is required for the site's dark/light mode toggle to function across page visits. This cannot be disabled without disabling that feature.

3. Analytics Cookies

If ABD RC enables a third-party analytics or advertising provider in the future, this policy will be updated to disclose the specific cookies used, their purpose and how to opt out.

4. Third-Party Content

Embedded content (such as a map on the Contact page, once connected) may set its own cookies in accordance with the third party's own policy.

5. Managing Storage

You can clear local storage and cookies at any time through your browser settings. Doing so will reset your theme preference to the default.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed via our Contact page.
