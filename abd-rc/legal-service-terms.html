Service Terms

Last updated: [Date placeholder]. These terms apply specifically to build, repair, manufacturing, training and consultancy engagements, in addition to our general Terms & Conditions.

1. Project Scope

All custom build, engineering, manufacturing and repair engagements are scoped in writing following a quote request or consultation. Work begins only after scope, timeline and (where applicable) cost are confirmed with the customer.

2. Customer-Supplied Materials & Designs

Where a customer supplies parts, materials or design files, ABD RC will assess suitability before starting work. We are not responsible for pre-existing defects in customer-supplied materials.

3. Flight Testing & Risk

RC aircraft, UAV and FPV operation carries inherent risk. Flight testing is conducted under structured safety protocols, but ABD RC cannot guarantee against all failure modes inherent to RC/UAV operation, including those caused by environmental conditions or third-party equipment.

4. Repair Assessments

Repair bookings begin with a diagnostic assessment. Where damage exceeds the scope of the original request, we will contact you before proceeding with additional work.

5. Training Programs

Training course content, schedules and any prerequisites are confirmed at booking. Certificates of completion, where offered, reflect ABD RC's own training record and are not a substitute for any regulatory or government pilot licensing that may apply in your jurisdiction.

6. Consultancy Advice

Consultancy recommendations are based on information provided by the customer and are intended to inform, not replace, your own due diligence and any applicable regulatory compliance review.

7. Cancellations & Rescheduling

Booking cancellations or rescheduling requests should be submitted as early as possible via Contact or the Customer Portal. [Specific cancellation policy to be confirmed by ABD RC.]

8. Contact

Questions about a specific engagement should reference your project or booking reference and be sent via our Contact page.
