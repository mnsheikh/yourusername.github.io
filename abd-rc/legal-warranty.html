Warranty Policy

Last updated: [Date placeholder]. Specific warranty terms are confirmed in writing at the time of quote or booking; this page summarizes our general approach.

1. Workmanship Coverage

ABD RC stands behind the workmanship of builds, repairs and manufactured components performed by our team. Coverage period and scope are confirmed in writing per project — see your quote or invoice for the specific terms that apply.

2. What Is Typically Covered

  • Assembly and fabrication defects directly attributable to our workmanship
  • Incorrect installation or configuration of electronics performed by our technicians
  • Structural failure at a point of repair, under normal operating conditions

3. What Is Typically Excluded

  • Crash damage or misuse after delivery
  • Wear items (batteries, propellers, control horns) under normal use
  • Modifications made by the customer or a third party after delivery
  • Defects in customer-supplied parts or materials
  • Damage from operating outside manufacturer or design specifications

4. Third-Party Components

Components sourced from third-party manufacturers (motors, ESCs, flight controllers, etc.) are covered under the original manufacturer's warranty where applicable, in addition to our workmanship coverage.

5. Making a Claim

To make a warranty claim, contact us via the Contact page or your Customer Portal with your project or repair reference, a description of the issue, and photos or video where possible.

6. Resolution

Valid claims are resolved through repair, replacement, or another remedy agreed with the customer, at ABD RC's discretion, consistent with the specific warranty terms confirmed for your project.
