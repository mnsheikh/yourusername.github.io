Overview

Precision mechanics, tuned flight

Helicopter performance is dominated by mechanical precision — swashplate geometry, blade tracking, and gyro tuning. We calibrate each of these individually rather than relying on default radio settings.

Every helicopter build is flight-tested for hover stability and control response before handover.
