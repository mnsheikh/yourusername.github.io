Overview

Built for how you actually fly

Whether you're training a new pilot, chasing top-end speed on a delta wing, or restoring a scale aircraft to flying condition, we start from mission requirements — payload, flight time, speed envelope — and engineer backward to airframe, power system and control setup.

Every build passes through CG verification, control-surface throw checks and a structured flight-test sequence before handover.
